[HOOK]

LinkedList should be faster for inserting elements than ArrayList.

That's what we learned, right?

But benchmark them...

and ArrayList can still win.

[WHY]

Here's the catch.

LinkedList insertion is cheap only after you're already at the correct node.

[SEARCH]

If you insert by index, LinkedList may first have to walk through the list to find that position.

ArrayList can access an index directly.

[REALITY]

And ArrayList stores elements close together, which is generally much friendlier to the CPU cache.

[RULE]

So don't memorize:

"Insertions? Use LinkedList."

The real answer is:

It depends on how you're accessing and inserting the data.

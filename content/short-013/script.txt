[HOOK]

Six numbers. One target.

Can you find the matching pair in just one pass?

[CHALLENGE]

Here's the array.

Thirteen, four, twenty one, eight, seventeen, and six.

Your target is twenty five.

Which two numbers add up to twenty five?

[REVEAL]

It's four and twenty one!

But here's the real interview trick.

How do you find them without checking every pair?

[WHY]

Use a HashMap.

For each number, subtract it from the target.

That tells you which number you need.

Check if you've already seen it.

[FIX]

When we reach twenty one, the map already contains four.

Match found!

And we only needed one pass.

[RULE]

Remember this.

Two Sum becomes faster when you store what you've already seen!

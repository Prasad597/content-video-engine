[HOOK]

Five numbers.

Four, one, two, one, two.

Every number appears twice, except one.

Find that number.

But no HashMap. No sorting.

And only constant extra space.

Can you solve it?

[REVEAL]

Here's the shortcut.

Use XOR.

XOR every number in the array.

[WHY]

A number XOR itself becomes zero.

And zero XOR any number gives that number back.

So duplicate pairs cancel each other.

[RESULT]

One XOR one becomes zero.

Two XOR two becomes zero.

Only four remains.

[RULE]

One loop.

Linear time.

Constant extra space.

Sometimes the trick isn't a data structure.

It's an operation.

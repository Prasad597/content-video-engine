Will this code compile?

Think about it.

Yes... it compiles.

But if b becomes zero at runtime...

ArithmeticException.

So why didn't the compiler force us to handle it?

Because this is an unchecked exception.

And the rule is actually very simple.

The compiler does NOT force you to catch an unchecked exception,
or declare it using throws.

Let's see what happens here.

getValue() returns zero.

Ten divided by zero...

ArithmeticException.

Now remember this for your interview:

RuntimeException and its subclasses are unchecked exceptions.

No mandatory catch or throws.
